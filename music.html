MUSIC

This is the page I use for tracks I see fit of sharing. Some will be older, others won't. Either way, This is my music page. Have fun, and enjoy listening to tracks by yours truly.

You can download all of these tracks (and more) here.

1. Memories

One of my early tracks, during the experimental phase. I really like how it ended up. I remember the original idea behind it - "A song with little to no repeated melodies." And I delivered with a track that I think still holds up to this day (mostly).

2. Insanity

A song that I made almost a year later, inspired my Memories. I think I did good, taking the cocept of Memories and completely changing the tone - This is a good one. It also has, like, 3 drum instruments playing at once for most of the song.

3. Breakdown

Another track inspired by Memories and Insanity - it delivers once again. This one was meant to be somber, but I eventually made it fast - and it worked. Amazingly, I might add. The main instrument I actually later use as BF's voice.

4. Untitled

An upbeat song I had no major ideas going into. But, yep, ended up in a similar vein as Memories, Insanity, and Breakdown. But it was all worth it. Seems to be the most recognized track of mine on Reddit. Move over, Chaos, we've got a new #1 track in town.

5. Chaos

""Former"" best track of mine. This was actually made before Insanity, but after Memories. It was the last of my tracks to be in the 7-8 minute range of length - at least, as of writing this. Still a great track.

6. Mischief

A fun lil' track made from a bug when trying to import a song made on the original BeepBox to AbyssBox - and Chaos was the song that was loaded beforehand. Also introduced me to the "Chip wave as a noise instrument" bug that I managed, and use in later tracks. It came after Chaos, but I think it came before Insanity? Can't exactly remember.

7. Chill

Just a chill, almost Lo-Fi sounding track I made for fun. Nothing to insane. I like how I used a square wave wit reverb for one of the instruments, though. I also like the chipwave music box.

8. Finale Remastered

A remaster to my second or third song, The Finale. It's kind of a mini-track, but not really. But, I will say, it's much better than the original.

9. The End.

A track that's basically, just: "What if a game of mine had a bad ending?" It uses the main melody of The Finale along with my Creativity Motif, plus a reference to Alice's motif. I even referenced "Neverending Night" by Toby Fox at the end of the track. It ended up being longer than I expected, too.
